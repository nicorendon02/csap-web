A Rich Heritage

More Than a Country

Colombia is a land of extraordinary diversity — from the lush Amazon rainforest and the snow-capped Andes to the golden Caribbean coast and the Pacific shores. It is the only country in South America with coastlines on both the Atlantic and Pacific oceans.

Home to the lost city of Ciudad Perdida, the magical town of Cartagena, and the world-famous Café de Colombia, the country's cultural richness is as diverse as its geography.

Colombia is also the birthplace of literary giant Gabriel García Márquez, the father of Magical Realism, whose works have captivated readers around the world.

Colombia's Regions

Caribbean Coast

Cartagena de Indias, Santa Marta, Barranquilla — colonial walls, clear waters, and Vallenato music.

Andes Region

Bogotá, Medellín, Cali — the heart of Colombia with coffee farms, museums, and vibrant nightlife.

Amazon Region

Leticia and Amazonas — one of the most biodiverse places on Earth, home to indigenous communities.

Pacific Coast

Chocó and Buenaventura — rich Afro-Colombian culture, whale watching, and pristine rainforests.

Orinoquía (Llanos)

Vast plains famous for wildlife, horseback riding, and the vibrant Llanera culture and music.

Islands

San Andrés and Providencia — Colombian Caribbean islands with crystal-clear waters and reef ecosystems.
