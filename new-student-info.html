First Steps

Arrival Checklist

  • Check in with ISS and complete your SEVIS/I-20 reporting
  • Get your Purdue ID card at the ID Card Office
  • Set up your Purdue email and BoilerKey two-factor authentication
  • Open a US bank account (many banks are near campus)
  • Get a local SIM card or US phone plan
  • Enroll in Purdue's health insurance or waive with proof of coverage
  • Register for your classes on MyPurdue
  • Attend CSAP's welcome event and meet the community!
  • Explore West Lafayette — coffee shops, grocery stores, restaurants
  • Download the Purdue app for maps, schedules, and alerts
