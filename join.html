Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to be Colombian to join?

Not at all! CSAP welcomes anyone who is interested in Colombian culture, has ties to Colombia, or simply wants to be part of a warm, vibrant community.

Is there a membership fee?

Membership is free! Some events may have a small fee to cover costs, but joining CSAP itself is always free of charge.

How often do you meet?

We hold general meetings each semester and organize multiple events throughout the year. Follow us on Instagram @csapurdue for the latest schedule.

Can I join mid-semester?

Absolutely! You can join CSAP at any time during the year. Just sign up on BoilerLink and come to our next event.
