Who We Are

A Home Away
from Home

Founded at Purdue University, the Colombian Student Association at Purdue (CSAP) is a vibrant community for Colombian students and those passionate about Colombian culture. We offer academic support, cultural events, networking opportunities, and a warm community that makes Purdue feel like home.
